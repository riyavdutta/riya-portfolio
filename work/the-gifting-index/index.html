    The Gifting Index

    Which perfume houses do people buy as gifts? A live site that answers from Google searches, drawn as six stems in one vase.

    LIVEgifting-index.vercel.app ↗
    WHAT
    A live site comparing three classic gift names (Jo Malone London, Diptyque, Molton Brown) with three cult niche houses (Le Labo, Byredo, Frédéric Malle)
    DATA
    Google Trends searches, fetched every morning and saved with the site, with Wikipedia pageviews as a cross-check

    THE QUESTION

    Some perfume names everyone recognises; others have an insider following. When people shop for someone else, does that difference show?

    THE CONSTRAINT

    No sales data from any brand. Only what’s public, and the answer had to keep itself up to date.

    THE FINDING

    The names people recognise jump every December.

    Searches for Jo Malone, Diptyque and Molton Brown roughly double each December. Le Labo, Byredo and Frédéric Malle rise by about half.

    Google searches in December against each year’s typical month, worldwide, every full year since 2016 (the niche houses from when Google has enough searches for them). The gift names were taller in all eight Decembers with data for both groups, 2018 to 2025. Figures as of November 2026; the live site recalculates them every day.

    KEEPING IT HONEST

    Checks that changed the story.

    Each of these made the site claim less than a first pass would have.

    1. I tested nine more houses before adding two. Penhaligon’s and Acqua di Parma behave like the niche houses, so “classic” here means the name people reach for as a present, not the oldest.

    2. An early pass found a March bump for Jo Malone that looked like Mother’s Day in the UK. Medians showed it was a few one-off spikes, so it was cut.

    3. A September spike for Jo Malone seemed to leave a +67% lasting lift. With the normal run-up to Christmas removed, it was +16%.

    4. The figures and the year count are calculated from the current data. If a niche house out-gifts the classics one year, the page says so.
