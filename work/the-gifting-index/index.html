The Gifting Index

Which luxury fragrance houses do people buy as gifts? A live site that answers from Google searches and ten years of Wikipedia data.

WHAT
A live site comparing two classic gift names, Jo Malone London and Diptyque, with two cult niche houses, Le Labo and Byredo
DATA
Google Trends searches (main signal) and Wikipedia pageviews, fetched every morning and saved with the site
MY PART
The question, the analysis, the writing, the design and the build
BUILT WITH
HTML, CSS and JavaScript; Python and a GitHub Action for the daily data

THE QUESTION

Two fragrance houses everyone recognises, two with an insider following. When people shop for someone else in December, does that difference show?

THE CONSTRAINT

No sales data from any brand. Only what’s public, and the answer had to keep itself up to date.

THE FINDING

Classics get gifted. Cult houses get chosen.

Every December, Google searches for Jo Malone and Diptyque roughly double. Le Labo and Byredo rise by about half.

The classic names lifted more in every December from 2018 to 2025, and Wikipedia pageviews show the same pattern. Figures as of November 2026; the live site recalculates them every day.

KEEPING IT HONEST

Checks that changed the story.

Each of these made the site claim less than a first pass would have, and the site shows its working.

  1. 01Two signals, not oneGoogle searches lean towards buying; Wikipedia views lean towards curiosity. The finding is reported because both agree.
  2. 02Medians, not averagesAn early pass found a March bump for Jo Malone that looked like Mother’s Day in the UK. Medians showed it was a few one-off spikes, so it was cut.
  3. 03Take the season outA September spike for Jo Malone seemed to leave a +67% lasting lift. With the normal run-up to Christmas removed, it was +16%.
  4. 04Let the data write the headlineThe headline and the year count are calculated from the current data. If the pattern breaks, the page says so.
THE SITE

Made to be used, not only read.

01Call itVisitors guess each house’s December lift before they see it. The finding stays sealed until they do.
02The live seasonThis year’s gifting season, day by day, against the range of past seasons. It updates until 6 January.
03Reliable by designA GitHub Action fetches the data every morning and saves it with the site, so a slow or failed source never breaks the page.
WHAT’S NEXT

This December is the live test. Byredo is running ahead of its usual season in both signals; if it holds, it would be the first year since 2018 that a cult house matches the classics.
