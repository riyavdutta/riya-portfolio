THE ASK

B2Ne needed a website. What it had was one customer, Nodinite, and no public presence at all: no site, no case studies, nothing a founder could find.

THE PROBLEM

A growth firm that couldn’t show its own growth. Nobody hands their growth to a firm that looks like it’s still figuring things out.

THE TURN

Stop claiming credibility. Demonstrate it.

One rule for every page: do something useful for a founder before they ever speak to us.

01A route, not a menuFounders at this stage rarely know which problem they have, so the site shows four milestones in order. A founder finds where they are and sees the next step.
02A calculator where the pitch should beThe question under every first call is cost. The Marketing Capacity Calculator answers what B2Ne saves against hiring, before anyone has to ask.
03Give the diagnosis awayWith no proof to show, the site offers expertise instead: a free 360° audit on request, ten pages on performance, SEO, competitors and paid spend, built from GTmetrix, PageSpeed, Ahrefs and SpyFu.
