The Haunted CRM

A Halloween campaign for B2Ne that told the everyday problems inside a sales team’s tools as horror stories.

CLIENT
B2Ne (Business To Nexus)
FOR
Founders and sales leads at small and mid-sized Nordic tech companies
RAN
6–10 Oct 2026, on LinkedIn
MADE
6 images, 7 carousels and 2 videos, plus a teaser, a trailer and a checklist
MY ROLE
Everything, end to end: concept, copy, design, video, posting and reading the results. I run B2Ne’s LinkedIn campaigns on my own.
THE BRIEF

Grow B2Ne’s organic audience on LinkedIn, and use the campaign as a test: how people react to different kinds of series, what they interact with, and when they’re most active.

THE AUDIENCE

Founders and sales leads at small and mid-sized tech companies in the Nordics: the people who own the contact list, the automatic emails, the sales spreadsheet and the open deals.

THE IDEA

Your sales tools are haunted.

Not by monsters. By the things nobody cleaned up.

01Ghost contactsPeople who stopped replying years ago, still marked “active”.
02Zombie emailsAutomatic emails nobody remembers setting up, still going out every Monday at 06:00.
03The cursed spreadsheetBuilt for three months, still holding the sales numbers years later.
04Dead dealsNo next step written down, still counted as open.
05The haunted inboxA customer’s message on the website contact form that nobody answers.

Every story ends where B2Ne starts: someone has to own the clean-up.

THE RULES

Checked line by line before anything shipped.

  1. 01PlainSomeone with no sales, marketing or tech background understands every word. No “pipeline”, no “workflow”, no “RevOps”.
  2. 02ConcreteName the exact thing: contact list, automatic email, sales spreadsheet, contact form. Never just “tools” or “process”.
  3. 03No invented statisticsNumbers are details of the story, like Deal #4471 or 06:00, never claims about the market.
  4. 04ShortOne headline and at most one short line per slide or scene. Cut words, not reading time.
THE WEEK

Five days, fifteen stamps.

Three posts a day at the same three times (Stockholm time), so every format and topic got tested in every slot. Pick a stamp to read what went out with it.

THE CAPTION

THE LEAD ASSET

Is your CRM haunted?

On the last day, the first comment carried a checklist of eleven symptoms. Ticked more than a few? DM “HAUNTED”. Every reply got the checklist with a short personal note: no pitch, and an easy way out.

The checklist names the symptoms, never the fixes. The fix is the conversation.

THE LOOK

Office things as horror props.
