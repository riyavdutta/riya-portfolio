THE PAPER

How ready are research labs to measure behaviour?

The paper looks at the measurement infrastructure available to consumer and behavioural research. It sets out five evaluative dimensions and a five-level maturity model, applies them across 32 research initiatives worldwide, and finds that most remain unequipped for multimodal behavioural measurement.
