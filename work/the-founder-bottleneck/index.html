The Founder Bottleneck

A week of LinkedIn posts about what stops when everything has to go through the founder.

CLIENT
B2Ne (Business To Nexus)
FOR
Founders of small and mid-sized B2B tech companies
RAN
12–16 Oct 2026, on LinkedIn
MADE
9 images, 2 carousels and 4 videos
MY ROLE
Everything, end to end: concept, copy, design, video, posting and reading the results. I run B2Ne’s LinkedIn campaigns on my own.
THE IDEA

The founder is the bottleneck.

Every proposal, renewal and customer call waits for one person, so the company only moves as fast as their calendar.

Each post shows one ordinary moment where that happens, and ends on the question a founder would rather not answer.

THE STRUCTURE

One week, three chapters.

Each chapter names a different cost, so the week builds from the founder’s evenings to their buyers to next year.

MON 12 – TUE 13 OCTIt costs your eveningsThe work that waits for you after hours: the open proposal, the holiday laptop, the sick day, the sleepless night.
WED 14 – THU 15 OCTIt costs your buyersA demo slot three weeks out, customers who only call you, a new customer explaining everything twice.
FRI 16 OCTIt costs your next yearThe new hire who has to “check with the founder”, and growth that adds jobs instead of handing them away.
THE WEEK

Fifteen pieces, in posting order.

Pick a stamp to see the post, play the video or swipe the carousel, and read the caption that went out with it.

Your team logged off hours ago. But the proposal, the pricing question and the follow-up only you can send are still open on your laptop. So dinner waits. Then the evening goes. The work didn't stop. It waited for you. What's still waiting on you tonight?
