    The Founder Bottleneck

    A week of LinkedIn posts about what stops when everything has to go through the founder.

    CLIENTB2Ne (Business To Nexus)RAN12–16 Oct 2026, on LinkedInMADE9 images, 2 carousels and 4 videos
    FOR
    Founders of small and mid-sized B2B tech companies
    MY ROLE
    Everything, end to end: concept, copy, design, video, posting and reading the results. I run B2Ne’s LinkedIn campaigns on my own.
    002

    THE IDEA

    The founder is the bottleneck.

    Every proposal, renewal and customer call waits for one person, so the company only moves as fast as their calendar.

    Each post shows one ordinary moment where that happens, and ends on the question a founder would rather not answer.

    THE STRUCTURE

    One week, three chapters.

    Each chapter names a different cost, so the week builds from the founder’s evenings to their buyers to next year.

    MON 12 – TUE 13 OCTIt costs your evenings

    The work that waits for you after hours: the open proposal, the holiday laptop, the sick day, the sleepless night.

    01
    WED 14 – THU 15 OCTIt costs your buyers

    A demo slot three weeks out, customers who only call you, a new customer explaining everything twice.

    02
    FRI 16 OCTIt costs your next year

    The new hire who has to “check with the founder”, and growth that adds jobs instead of handing them away.

    Lamp light against laptop light.

    Night scenes throughout: warm amber lamp light against the cold blue of a laptop, so the cost always lands after hours.
